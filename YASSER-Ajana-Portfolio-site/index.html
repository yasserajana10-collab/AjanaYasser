About me

Senior graphic designer, motion designer and art director. I build brands that move, on screen and on two wheels.

Since June 2026 I'm a Graphic Designer and Motion Designer at Belocum, where I design and animate the app's brand and interface content. Before that I spent four years as an Engineer in Graphic Design at Alten Morocco, worked with agencies like 212 Communication, and have freelanced for my own clients since 2019. I like owning a project end to end: the strategy, the look, the animation and the final edit.

Off the clock I ride. Long solo trips taught me to plan the route, respect the conditions and commit to the line, and that's the way I run every brief.
